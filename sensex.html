WHY THIS IS DIFFERENT

Not a chatbot with a camera. A closed loop, on the device.

The difference isn't a feature — it's where the reasoning happens and how many signals it uses.

TRADITIONAL APPROACH

User records one signal — usually just a photo or video clip.
File is uploaded to the cloud and queued for processing.
General-purpose model gives a vague, generic visual description.
No confidence score, no cause — user is left to interpret it themselves.
Requires network connectivity and raises data privacy questions.
LATENCY: NETWORK-DEPENDENT · SIGNALS: 1

SENSEX APPROACH

Captures camera + mic + motion together, time-aligned.
Everything is analysed locally, on-device — no upload, no wait.
Signals are cross-checked against each other, not read in isolation.
Outputs a specific cause, confidence score, and next inspection step.
Works fully offline, keeping raw sensor data private to the device.
LATENCY: ON-DEVICE, SECONDS · SIGNALS: 3, FUSED

TECHNICAL CREDIBILITY

What's actually running underneath.

A compact, honest look at the stack — expand any layer for detail.

01Frontend / capture app
Native Android capture layer coordinating camera, microphone and IMU streams with a shared clock, plus the guided-capture and results UI.
02On-device AI / ML
Lightweight on-device models for visual motion analysis, acoustic spectral classification and vibration pattern matching, combined in a fusion layer that produces the ranked diagnosis.
03Local data & storage
Fault signatures and past scan history are stored locally on-device, so diagnoses remain available without connectivity.
04Backend & sync (optional)
A minimal backend exists only for optional fault-signature library updates and multi-device history sync — never required for a diagnosis to run.
05Security & privacy
Raw camera, audio and motion data never leave the device by default — only the final diagnosis is ever eligible for optional sync.
06Integration & APIs
Designed with a clean interface boundary so the fusion engine can later plug into maintenance, education, or fleet-management tools.
